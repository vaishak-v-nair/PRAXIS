# PRAXIS full-screen revision — 2026-10-03

The user accepted the narration and overall story. This pass keeps that audio,
correct/broken demo pair and real product behavior. It addresses a small cropped
video panel, soft enlarged UI, and motion that does not guide attention enough.

## Three approaches

1. Mascot story: memorable, but too much illustration would hide the tool.
2. Cinematic brand ad: dramatic light and abstract engine motion; less useful
   for learning the actual workflow and liable to imply unbuilt capabilities.
3. Immersive walkthrough: real UI occupies the complete canvas, with measured
   camera moves to the control, finding or test being discussed. Selected.

## The visual idea

Looks finished. Look closer. The axolotl inspects a project outline; the outline
opens all the way into the live product. We then follow one order-saving bug
through intake, specialist reviews, real Docker tests, a finding and a plan.
The PASS/FAIL comparison is the turning point. The plan is the useful outcome.
We return to the mascot and one clear invitation to try PRAXIS.

## Framing and movement

Middle scenes are edge-to-edge 16:9 recordings, rather than an embedded card.
No black side bars, outer matte or permanent title band. Leave whole controls,
important caveats and test output visible. Use restrained, seekable camera moves
only to follow an action or enlarge the proof. Hold the camera while someone
reads. Do not paint a simulated UI, fabricate a backend result or add badges.

Use original project-sheet/lens motion in the opening and closing. A thin
inspection outline may guide the eye to a real control; its geometry must trace
to a measured locator. Chapter headings belong in navigation context or brief
transitions, not layered over the app's own headings. No decorative stars.

## Sharpness

Probe browser capture at a higher device pixel density first. Verify actual
recorded pixel dimensions and content coverage; a 4K filename or encoder size
alone does not prove sharpness. Recapture original UI, not upscale the old
1224x502 recording. Preserve original takes, source fingerprints and spend.
Render a high-quality full-HD distribution file and a 4K master only if native
capture and the renderer preserve its real detail. Test the actual encoded file.

## Captions and sound

Retain the user-approved conversational narration. Keep sentence captions in
a consistent, readable safe zone; verify that the important product elements
stay outside that zone throughout each settled shot. Provide a subtitle sidecar.
Do not add loud music or unrelated sound effects merely to create activity.

## Product versus film

The user approved the dashboard redesign. Baseline contracts were pressure-tested
before the inspection-workspace implementation. Record the revised app after its
regressions pass. A film cannot make a dashboard better by hiding unavailable
providers or skipped checks.

## Completion

Full-frame product pixels, legible text, deliberate movement, readable proof,
unchanged narration and honest capability boundaries. Test captions, geometry,
seeking, full decode, audio peaks and actual resolution. Independent visual
review is required; do not call sampled review exhaustive or award-winning.
