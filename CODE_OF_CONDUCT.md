# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue with the idea, never the person.

This project is small and public, and the bar for participating is simply that
other people are glad you did.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and native
  languages. Not everyone here writes English as a first language, and not
  everyone has been programming for a decade.
- Give feedback on the work, with a reason. "This breaks X because Y" is useful;
  "this is bad" is not.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no. A rejected PR is a scoping decision,
  not a verdict on you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind, including sexualized content.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately bad-faith argument.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **vaishak.v.nair.dev@gmail.com**.
Reports are read by the maintainer only, handled confidentially, and answered.
You will not be asked to justify why something bothered you.

Responses range from a private word, to edited or removed content, to a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces — proportionate to what happened
and whether it continued after being raised.
